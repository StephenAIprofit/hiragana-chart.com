Hiragana dakuten and handakuten

Dakuten add two dots (が ざ だ ば). Handakuten add a circle (ぱ ぴ ぷ ぺ ぽ). In modern Japanese, ぢ is read ji and づ is read zu.

Hiragana yoon (contracted sounds)

Yoon is a large kana plus a small ゃ, ゅ, or ょ. The small character is not an independent sound. ぢゃ, ぢゅ, and ぢょ are rare; they use the same sound as じゃ, じゅ, and じょ.

Sokuon & Long Vowels

Sokuon っ / ッ doubles the next consonant. Tap an example to hear it.

Long vowels: Katakana uses ー (コーヒー, コンピューター). Hiragana uses おう, おお, or えい (おかあさん, とおい, せんせい).

Hiragana pronunciation guide

Read the chart in gojūon order: each row adds a consonant to the five vowels あ (a), い (i), う (u), え (e), お (o). Say them "ah, ee, oo, eh, oh" — short and even, never stressed. か is "ka", き is "ki", and so on across the row.

Five readings trip up beginners: し is shi (not "si"), ち is chi (not "ti"), つ is tsu (not "tu"), ふ is fu — a soft blow between "h" and "f", and を is written wo but sounds like "o". ん is a syllabic "n" that hums through the nose. Click any character above to hear it.

In real sentences three characters change sound as particles: は is read wa, へ is read e, and を is read o. On this chart you see their dictionary readings: ha, he, wo.

Three tips that actually work

  1. Learn one row a day in gojūon order — あ行, か行, さ行 — and say each character out loud as you write it.
  2. Write by hand and follow the stroke order above; your hand remembers what your eyes forget.
  3. Test yourself backwards (o, e, u, i, a) and shuffled. If you only know あ→お order, you don't know them yet.

Common questions

How many characters are on a Hiragana or Katakana chart?

46 basic characters, from あ to ん in gojuon order. The obsolete ゐ and ゑ are not included. Use the switcher to see the same 46 in Katakana.

What Romaji does this chart use?

Hepburn. し is shi, ち is chi, つ is tsu, ふ is fu, and the particle を is written wo.

How do you pronounce は, へ, and を in sentences?

As particles: は is "wa", へ is "e", and を is "o".

What is the difference between Hiragana and Katakana?

Hiragana writes native Japanese words and grammar. Katakana writes foreign loanwords and emphasis. Same 46 sounds, different scripts.

How long does it take to learn the 46 characters?

Most learners memorize all 46 in one to two weeks at one row a day, then need more time to read them quickly. Use the quiz to practice.

How do I see stroke order?

Click any character. The diagram shows each stroke in order. Yoon shows the base character first, then the small kana.

Does the page play audio automatically?

No. Audio plays only when you click a character or a sokuon example.
