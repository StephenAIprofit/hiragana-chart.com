About

Why I created this site

When I started learning Japanese, the charts I found were just tables. I could see the characters, but I could not hear them, and I could not see how they were written. I ended up memorizing shapes or replaying recordings. That was slow, and it did not stick.

I wanted to hear a character the moment I clicked it, and watch the strokes in order. So I built the chart. Then I wanted to test myself without opening another site, so I added the Quiz. Later I needed to look up Kanji I could not type, so I added the Translator with a drawing pad.

Western learners of Japanese face an extra step that native readers do not: they must learn Kanji from scratch. A table of kana is not enough. This site puts pronunciation, stroke order, practice, and Kanji lookup in one place, so a learner does not have to jump between tools. I made it because those were the problems I hit, and I wanted one tool that solved them.
