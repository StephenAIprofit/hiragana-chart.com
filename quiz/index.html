Quiz questions

What are the two Quiz modes?

Characters mode shows a reading or a kana and asks you to pick the match. Words mode shows a simple English word and asks you to pick the correct kana spelling. Example: school is がっこう. Use Characters mode to learn recognition, and Words mode to learn spelling.

What do L1, L2, L3, and L4 mean?

L1 is the basic 46. L2 adds Dakuten and Handakuten, for 71. L3 adds Yoon, for 107. L4 is the same set as L3, not a separate set. Start at L1, and move up only when you can pass the current level without looking at the chart.

How do score, combo, and accuracy work?

Each correct answer adds to your score and combo. A wrong answer breaks the combo and shows the correct kana with its sound. Accuracy is your correct answers as a share of all answers in the current round. Use combo to check fluency, not just memory: a high score with a broken combo usually means you still hesitate on a few characters.

Do wrong answers come back, and do I need an account?

No account is needed. Wrong answers return later in the same session for review. Nothing is saved to a server, and progress does not sync across devices. If you start a new session, start a new round and focus on the characters you missed last time.

How do I practice Katakana in the Quiz?

Select the Katakana tab on the same page. The levels stay the same: L1 to L4 in Katakana mirror the Hiragana levels, and L4 in Katakana is also the same set as L3 in Katakana. If you keep missing the same Katakana pair, go back to the Katakana version on the chart and compare the pair side by side before you retest.

What should I do after I finish a Quiz round?

Do not just start another round. Go back to the chart section for the set you missed most, for example Basic 46, Dakuten, or Yoon, write those characters once by hand using the Printable chart, then retest them shuffled. If you only know characters in gojuon order, test them backwards as well.
