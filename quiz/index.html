Quiz questions

How does this quiz work?

Each round covers the selected set in random order. Pick the matching character or reading. A correct answer adds a combo. A wrong answer shows the correct kana, plays the sound, and saves the item for review.

What is the Words mode?

Words mode shows a simple English word. Pick the correct kana spelling. Example: school is がっこう. This is the hiragana words quiz.

What are the four levels?

L1 is the basic 46. L2 adds dakuten and handakuten (71). L3 adds yoon (107). L4 is the full set. Katakana uses the same levels.

What Romaji does the quiz use?

Hepburn Romaji. し is shi, ち is chi, つ is tsu, ふ is fu, and を is written wo.

What is Katakana used for?

Katakana writes foreign loanwords, names, and emphasis. The sounds match Hiragana. Switch the tab to practice Katakana.

How do I practice Katakana?

Select the Katakana tab. The title, questions, and FAQ switch to Katakana. Use the chart at Katakana version to review.

Do wrong answers come back?

Yes. A wrong answer is saved for review and returns later in the same session.

How this quiz works

Characters mode shows a reading or a kana and asks you to pick the match. Words mode shows an English word and asks for the kana spelling. Wrong answers return later in the same round. Switch to Katakana to practice the same levels in the other script. Your score stays in this browser.
