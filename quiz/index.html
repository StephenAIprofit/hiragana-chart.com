How this hiragana quiz works

Each round covers the 46 modern hiragana characters in random order. Pick the matching character or reading. A correct answer adds a combo. A wrong answer shows the correct kana, plays the sound, and saves the item for review.

Romaji follows Hepburn: し is shi, ち is chi, つ is tsu, ふ is fu, and を is written wo. Use the hiragana chart to hear a character and watch its stroke order before you quiz.
