Hiragana みゅ (myu)

みゅ is a yoon: み (mi) plus a small ゅ. The two characters are read together as one beat, myu. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

みゅ myu

4 strokes

How to write みゅ: stroke order

み (2 strokes)

small ゅ (2 strokes)

Write み at full size, then the small ゅ in the lower right of the same square, at about half the size. Total: 4 strokes.

How to pronounce みゅ

Say it as one sound, not mi plus yu. Compare みゅ (myu, one beat) with みゆ (mi-yu, two beats).

Is みゅ common?

みゅ is rare in modern Hiragana. Learn to recognise it, but spend your practice time on the common sounds first.
