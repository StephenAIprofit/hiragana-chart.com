Hiragana ふ (fu)

ふ sits in the ha row, u column of the gojuon chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ふ fu

4 strokes

How to write ふ: stroke order

ふ has 4 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

Practise writing ふ by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

Get the Workbook · $9.90

How to pronounce ふ

Not an English f. Blow air gently between both lips, as if cooling soup. It sits between h and f.
