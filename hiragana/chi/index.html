Hiragana ち (chi)

ち sits in the ta row, i column of the gojuon chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ち chi

2 strokes

How to write ち: stroke order

ち has 2 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

Practise writing ち by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce ち

Hepburn writes chi, not ti. Close to the chee in cheese, short.
