Hiragana ざ (za)

ざ is さ with two dots (dakuten) added. The mark changes the sound from sa to za. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ざ za

5 strokes

How to write ざ: stroke order

ざ has 5 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next. Write さ first, then add the two dots in the top right.

Practise writing ざ by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce ざ

za is the z sound followed by a short, pure a. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
