Hiragana あ (a)

あ is one of the five vowels, in the first row of the chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

あ a

3 strokes

How to write あ: stroke order

あ has 3 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

Practise writing あ by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce あ

A short, open a, like the a in father but clipped. Japanese vowels are pure: no glide at the end.
