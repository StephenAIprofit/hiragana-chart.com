Hiragana わ (wa)

わ sits in the wa row, a column of the gojuon chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

わ wa

2 strokes

How to write わ: stroke order

わ has 2 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

Practise writing わ by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce わ

wa is the w sound followed by a short, pure a. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
