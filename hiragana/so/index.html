Hiragana そ (so)

そ sits in the sa row, o column of the gojuon chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

そ so

1 stroke

How to write そ: stroke order

そ has 1 stroke. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

Practise writing そ by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce そ

so is the s sound followed by a short, pure o. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
