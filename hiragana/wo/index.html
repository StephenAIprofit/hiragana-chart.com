Hiragana を (wo)

を sits in the wa row, o column of the gojuon chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

を wo

3 strokes

How to write を: stroke order

を has 3 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

Practise writing を by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce を

Written wo, but read o in modern Japanese. It is almost only used as the object particle: ほんをよむ (hon o yomu).
