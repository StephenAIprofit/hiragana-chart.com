Hiragana き (ki)

き sits in the ka row, i column of the gojuon chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

き ki

4 strokes

How to write き: stroke order

き has 4 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

Practise writing き by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

Get the Workbook · $9.90

How to pronounce き

ki is the k sound followed by a short, pure i. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
