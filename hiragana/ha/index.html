Hiragana は (ha)

は sits in the ha row, a column of the gojuon chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

は ha

3 strokes

How to write は: stroke order

は has 3 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

How to pronounce は

Read ha in words, but as the topic particle は it is read wa: わたしは (watashi wa).
