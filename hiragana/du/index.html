Hiragana づ (zu)

づ is つ with two dots (dakuten) added. The mark changes the sound from tsu to zu. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

づ zu

3 strokes

How to write づ: stroke order

づ has 3 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next. Write つ first, then add the two dots in the top right.

Practise writing づ by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce づ

Read zu, the same sound as ず. It appears where つ becomes voiced, such as つづく (tsuzuku). When typing, use du.
