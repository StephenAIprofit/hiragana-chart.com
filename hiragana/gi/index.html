Hiragana ぎ (gi)

ぎ is き with two dots (dakuten) added. The mark changes the sound from ki to gi. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ぎ gi

6 strokes

How to write ぎ: stroke order

ぎ has 6 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next. Write き first, then add the two dots in the top right.

How to pronounce ぎ

gi is the g sound followed by a short, pure i. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
