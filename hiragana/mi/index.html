Hiragana み (mi)

み sits in the ma row, i column of the gojuon chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

み mi

2 strokes

How to write み: stroke order

み has 2 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

Practise writing み by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce み

mi is the m sound followed by a short, pure i. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
