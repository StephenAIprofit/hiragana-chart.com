Hiragana る (ru)

る sits in the ra row, u column of the gojuon chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

る ru

1 stroke

How to write る: stroke order

る has 1 stroke. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

Practise writing る by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce る

Japanese r is a quick tap of the tongue tip behind the teeth, between English r, l, and d.
