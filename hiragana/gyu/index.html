Hiragana ぎゅ (gyu)

ぎゅ is a yoon: ぎ (gi) plus a small ゅ. The two characters are read together as one beat, gyu. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ぎゅ gyu

8 strokes

How to write ぎゅ: stroke order

ぎ (6 strokes)

small ゅ (2 strokes)

Write ぎ at full size, then the small ゅ in the lower right of the same square, at about half the size. Total: 8 strokes.

Practise writing ぎゅ by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce ぎゅ

Say it as one sound, not gi plus yu. Compare ぎゅ (gyu, one beat) with ぎゆ (gi-yu, two beats).
