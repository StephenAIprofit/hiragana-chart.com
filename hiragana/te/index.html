Hiragana て (te)

て sits in the ta row, e column of the gojuon chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

て te

1 stroke

How to write て: stroke order

て has 1 stroke. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

How to pronounce て

te is the t sound followed by a short, pure e. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
