Hiragana ぐ (gu)

ぐ is く with two dots (dakuten) added. The mark changes the sound from ku to gu. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ぐ gu

3 strokes

How to write ぐ: stroke order

ぐ has 3 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next. Write く first, then add the two dots in the top right.

Practise writing ぐ by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce ぐ

gu is the g sound followed by a short, pure u. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
