Hiragana しゅ (shu)

しゅ is a yoon: し (shi) plus a small ゅ. The two characters are read together as one beat, shu. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

しゅ shu

3 strokes

How to write しゅ: stroke order

し (1 strokes)

small ゅ (2 strokes)

Write し at full size, then the small ゅ in the lower right of the same square, at about half the size. Total: 3 strokes.

Practise writing しゅ by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce しゅ

Say it as one sound, not shi plus yu. Compare しゅ (shu, one beat) with しゆ (shi-yu, two beats).
