Hiragana ら (ra)

ら sits in the ra row, a column of the gojuon chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ら ra

2 strokes

How to write ら: stroke order

ら has 2 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

How to pronounce ら

Japanese r is a quick tap of the tongue tip behind the teeth, between English r, l, and d.
