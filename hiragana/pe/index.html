Hiragana ぺ (pe)

ぺ is へ with a small circle (handakuten) added. The mark changes the sound from he to pe. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ぺ pe

2 strokes

How to write ぺ: stroke order

ぺ has 2 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next. Write へ first, then add the circle in the top right.

Practise writing ぺ by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce ぺ

pe is the p sound followed by a short, pure e. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
