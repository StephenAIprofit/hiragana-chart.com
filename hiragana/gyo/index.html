Hiragana ぎょ (gyo)

ぎょ is a yoon: ぎ (gi) plus a small ょ. The two characters are read together as one beat, gyo. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ぎょ gyo

8 strokes

How to write ぎょ: stroke order

ぎ (6 strokes)

small ょ (2 strokes)

Write ぎ at full size, then the small ょ in the lower right of the same square, at about half the size. Total: 8 strokes.

Practise writing ぎょ by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce ぎょ

Say it as one sound, not gi plus yo. Compare ぎょ (gyo, one beat) with ぎよ (gi-yo, two beats).
