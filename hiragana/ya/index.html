Hiragana や (ya)

や sits in the ya row, a column of the gojuon chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

や ya

3 strokes

How to write や: stroke order

や has 3 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

Practise writing や by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce や

ya is the y sound followed by a short, pure a. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
