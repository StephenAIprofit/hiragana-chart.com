Hiragana く (ku)

く sits in the ka row, u column of the gojuon chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

く ku

1 stroke

How to write く: stroke order

く has 1 stroke. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

Practise writing く by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce く

ku is the k sound followed by a short, pure u. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
