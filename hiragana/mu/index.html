Hiragana む (mu)

む sits in the ma row, u column of the gojuon chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

む mu

3 strokes

How to write む: stroke order

む has 3 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

Practise writing む by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce む

mu is the m sound followed by a short, pure u. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
