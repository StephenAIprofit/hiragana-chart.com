Hiragana ぎゃ (gya)

ぎゃ is a yoon: ぎ (gi) plus a small ゃ. The two characters are read together as one beat, gya. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ぎゃ gya

9 strokes

How to write ぎゃ: stroke order

ぎ (6 strokes)

small ゃ (3 strokes)

Write ぎ at full size, then the small ゃ in the lower right of the same square, at about half the size. Total: 9 strokes.

Practise writing ぎゃ by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce ぎゃ

Say it as one sound, not gi plus ya. Compare ぎゃ (gya, one beat) with ぎや (gi-ya, two beats).
