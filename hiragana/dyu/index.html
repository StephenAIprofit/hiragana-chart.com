Hiragana ぢゅ (ju)

ぢゅ is a yoon: ぢ (ji) plus a small ゅ. The two characters are read together as one beat, ju. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ぢゅ ju

6 strokes

How to write ぢゅ: stroke order

ぢ (4 strokes)

small ゅ (2 strokes)

Write ぢ at full size, then the small ゅ in the lower right of the same square, at about half the size. Total: 6 strokes.

Practise writing ぢゅ by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce ぢゅ

Read ju, the same as じゅ. This spelling is rare in modern Japanese.

Is ぢゅ common?

ぢゅ is rare in modern Hiragana. Learn to recognise it, but spend your practice time on the common sounds first.
