Hiragana ず (zu)

ず is す with two dots (dakuten) added. The mark changes the sound from su to zu. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ず zu

4 strokes

How to write ず: stroke order

ず has 4 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next. Write す first, then add the two dots in the top right.

Practise writing ず by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce ず

In modern Japanese ず and づ sound the same. ず is the normal spelling; づ appears in words like つづく.
