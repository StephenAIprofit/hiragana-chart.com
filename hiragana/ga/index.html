Hiragana が (ga)

が is か with two dots (dakuten) added. The mark changes the sound from ka to ga. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

が ga

5 strokes

How to write が: stroke order

が has 5 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next. Write か first, then add the two dots in the top right.

Practise writing が by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce が

ga is the g sound followed by a short, pure a. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
