Hiragana ゆ (yu)

ゆ sits in the ya row, u column of the gojuon chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ゆ yu

2 strokes

How to write ゆ: stroke order

ゆ has 2 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

How to pronounce ゆ

yu is the y sound followed by a short, pure u. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
