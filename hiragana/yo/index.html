Hiragana よ (yo)

よ sits in the ya row, o column of the gojuon chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

よ yo

2 strokes

How to write よ: stroke order

よ has 2 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

How to pronounce よ

yo is the y sound followed by a short, pure o. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
