Hiragana ぷ (pu)

ぷ is ふ with a small circle (handakuten) added. The mark changes the sound from fu to pu. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ぷ pu

5 strokes

How to write ぷ: stroke order

ぷ has 5 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next. Write ふ first, then add the circle in the top right.

Practise writing ぷ by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce ぷ

pu is the p sound followed by a short, pure u. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
