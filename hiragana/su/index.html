Hiragana す (su)

す sits in the sa row, u column of the gojuon chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

す su

2 strokes

How to write す: stroke order

す has 2 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

Practise writing す by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce す

su is the s sound followed by a short, pure u. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
