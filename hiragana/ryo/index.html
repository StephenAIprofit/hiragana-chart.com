Hiragana りょ (ryo)

りょ is a yoon: り (ri) plus a small ょ. The two characters are read together as one beat, ryo. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

りょ ryo

4 strokes

How to write りょ: stroke order

り (2 strokes)

small ょ (2 strokes)

Write り at full size, then the small ょ in the lower right of the same square, at about half the size. Total: 4 strokes.

Practise writing りょ by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce りょ

Say it as one sound, not ri plus yo. Compare りょ (ryo, one beat) with りよ (ri-yo, two beats).
