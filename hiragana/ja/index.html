Hiragana じゃ (ja)

じゃ is a yoon: じ (ji) plus a small ゃ. The two characters are read together as one beat, ja. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

じゃ ja

6 strokes

How to write じゃ: stroke order

じ (3 strokes)

small ゃ (3 strokes)

Write じ at full size, then the small ゃ in the lower right of the same square, at about half the size. Total: 6 strokes.

Practise writing じゃ by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce じゃ

Say it as one sound, not ji plus ya. Compare じゃ (ja, one beat) with じや (ji-ya, two beats).
