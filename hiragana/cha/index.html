Hiragana ちゃ (cha)

ちゃ is a yoon: ち (chi) plus a small ゃ. The two characters are read together as one beat, cha. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ちゃ cha

5 strokes

How to write ちゃ: stroke order

ち (2 strokes)

small ゃ (3 strokes)

Write ち at full size, then the small ゃ in the lower right of the same square, at about half the size. Total: 5 strokes.

Practise writing ちゃ by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce ちゃ

Say it as one sound, not chi plus ya. Compare ちゃ (cha, one beat) with ちや (chi-ya, two beats).
