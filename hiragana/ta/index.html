Hiragana た (ta)

た sits in the ta row, a column of the gojuon chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

た ta

4 strokes

How to write た: stroke order

た has 4 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

Practise writing た by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce た

ta is the t sound followed by a short, pure a. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
