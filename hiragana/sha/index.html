Hiragana しゃ (sha)

しゃ is a yoon: し (shi) plus a small ゃ. The two characters are read together as one beat, sha. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

しゃ sha

4 strokes

How to write しゃ: stroke order

し (1 strokes)

small ゃ (3 strokes)

Write し at full size, then the small ゃ in the lower right of the same square, at about half the size. Total: 4 strokes.

Practise writing しゃ by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce しゃ

Say it as one sound, not shi plus ya. Compare しゃ (sha, one beat) with しや (shi-ya, two beats).
