Hiragana りゃ (rya)

りゃ is a yoon: り (ri) plus a small ゃ. The two characters are read together as one beat, rya. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

りゃ rya

5 strokes

How to write りゃ: stroke order

り (2 strokes)

small ゃ (3 strokes)

Write り at full size, then the small ゃ in the lower right of the same square, at about half the size. Total: 5 strokes.

Practise writing りゃ by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce りゃ

Say it as one sound, not ri plus ya. Compare りゃ (rya, one beat) with りや (ri-ya, two beats).
