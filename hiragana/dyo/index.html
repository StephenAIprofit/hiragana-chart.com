Hiragana ぢょ (jo)

ぢょ is a yoon: ぢ (ji) plus a small ょ. The two characters are read together as one beat, jo. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ぢょ jo

6 strokes

How to write ぢょ: stroke order

ぢ (4 strokes)

small ょ (2 strokes)

Write ぢ at full size, then the small ょ in the lower right of the same square, at about half the size. Total: 6 strokes.

How to pronounce ぢょ

Read jo, the same as じょ. This spelling is rare in modern Japanese.

Is ぢょ common?

ぢょ is rare in modern Hiragana. Learn to recognise it, but spend your practice time on the common sounds first.
