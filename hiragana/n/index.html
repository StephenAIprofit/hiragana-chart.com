Hiragana ん (n)

ん is the last character of the chart and stands on its own row. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ん n

1 stroke

How to write ん: stroke order

ん has 1 stroke. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

How to pronounce ん

The only kana that is just a consonant. It takes one full beat: にほん is three beats, ni-ho-n. Before p, b, and m it sounds like m.
