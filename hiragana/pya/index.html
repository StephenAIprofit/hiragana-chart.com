Hiragana ぴゃ (pya)

ぴゃ is a yoon: ぴ (pi) plus a small ゃ. The two characters are read together as one beat, pya. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ぴゃ pya

5 strokes

How to write ぴゃ: stroke order

ぴ (2 strokes)

small ゃ (3 strokes)

Write ぴ at full size, then the small ゃ in the lower right of the same square, at about half the size. Total: 5 strokes.

How to pronounce ぴゃ

Say it as one sound, not pi plus ya. Compare ぴゃ (pya, one beat) with ぴや (pi-ya, two beats).
