Hiragana ぞ (zo)

ぞ is そ with two dots (dakuten) added. The mark changes the sound from so to zo. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ぞ zo

3 strokes

How to write ぞ: stroke order

ぞ has 3 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next. Write そ first, then add the two dots in the top right.

Practise writing ぞ by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce ぞ

zo is the z sound followed by a short, pure o. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
