Hiragana う (u)

う is one of the five vowels, in the first row of the chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

う u

2 strokes

How to write う: stroke order

う has 2 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

Practise writing う by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce う

An unrounded u: say oo with relaxed, not pushed-out, lips. After k and s it often goes quiet, as in です (desu, sounds like dess).
