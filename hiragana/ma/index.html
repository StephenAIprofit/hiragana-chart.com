Hiragana ま (ma)

ま sits in the ma row, a column of the gojuon chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ま ma

3 strokes

How to write ま: stroke order

ま has 3 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

Practise writing ま by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce ま

ma is the m sound followed by a short, pure a. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
