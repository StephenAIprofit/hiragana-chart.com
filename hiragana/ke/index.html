Hiragana け (ke)

け sits in the ka row, e column of the gojuon chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

け ke

3 strokes

How to write け: stroke order

け has 3 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

Practise writing け by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce け

ke is the k sound followed by a short, pure e. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
