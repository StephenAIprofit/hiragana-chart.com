Hiragana ちょ (cho)

ちょ is a yoon: ち (chi) plus a small ょ. The two characters are read together as one beat, cho. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ちょ cho

4 strokes

How to write ちょ: stroke order

ち (2 strokes)

small ょ (2 strokes)

Write ち at full size, then the small ょ in the lower right of the same square, at about half the size. Total: 4 strokes.

Practise writing ちょ by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce ちょ

Say it as one sound, not chi plus yo. Compare ちょ (cho, one beat) with ちよ (chi-yo, two beats).
