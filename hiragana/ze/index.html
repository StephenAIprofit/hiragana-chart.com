Hiragana ぜ (ze)

ぜ is せ with two dots (dakuten) added. The mark changes the sound from se to ze. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ぜ ze

5 strokes

How to write ぜ: stroke order

ぜ has 5 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next. Write せ first, then add the two dots in the top right.

Practise writing ぜ by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce ぜ

ze is the z sound followed by a short, pure e. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
