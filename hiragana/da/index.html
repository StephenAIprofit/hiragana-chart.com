Hiragana だ (da)

だ is た with two dots (dakuten) added. The mark changes the sound from ta to da. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

だ da

6 strokes

How to write だ: stroke order

だ has 6 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next. Write た first, then add the two dots in the top right.

How to pronounce だ

da is the d sound followed by a short, pure a. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
