Hiragana ひょ (hyo)

ひょ is a yoon: ひ (hi) plus a small ょ. The two characters are read together as one beat, hyo. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ひょ hyo

3 strokes

How to write ひょ: stroke order

ひ (1 strokes)

small ょ (2 strokes)

Write ひ at full size, then the small ょ in the lower right of the same square, at about half the size. Total: 3 strokes.

How to pronounce ひょ

Say it as one sound, not hi plus yo. Compare ひょ (hyo, one beat) with ひよ (hi-yo, two beats).
