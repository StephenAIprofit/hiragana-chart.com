Hiragana ひゃ (hya)

ひゃ is a yoon: ひ (hi) plus a small ゃ. The two characters are read together as one beat, hya. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ひゃ hya

4 strokes

How to write ひゃ: stroke order

ひ (1 strokes)

small ゃ (3 strokes)

Write ひ at full size, then the small ゃ in the lower right of the same square, at about half the size. Total: 4 strokes.

Practise writing ひゃ by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce ひゃ

Say it as one sound, not hi plus ya. Compare ひゃ (hya, one beat) with ひや (hi-ya, two beats).
