Hiragana じゅ (ju)

じゅ is a yoon: じ (ji) plus a small ゅ. The two characters are read together as one beat, ju. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

じゅ ju

5 strokes

How to write じゅ: stroke order

じ (3 strokes)

small ゅ (2 strokes)

Write じ at full size, then the small ゅ in the lower right of the same square, at about half the size. Total: 5 strokes.

How to pronounce じゅ

Say it as one sound, not ji plus yu. Compare じゅ (ju, one beat) with じゆ (ji-yu, two beats).
