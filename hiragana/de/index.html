Hiragana で (de)

で is て with two dots (dakuten) added. The mark changes the sound from te to de. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

で de

3 strokes

How to write で: stroke order

で has 3 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next. Write て first, then add the two dots in the top right.

Practise writing で by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce で

de is the d sound followed by a short, pure e. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
