Hiragana ぢ (ji)

ぢ is ち with two dots (dakuten) added. The mark changes the sound from chi to ji. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ぢ ji

4 strokes

How to write ぢ: stroke order

ぢ has 4 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next. Write ち first, then add the two dots in the top right.

How to pronounce ぢ

Read ji, the same sound as じ. It appears mainly in compounds where ち becomes voiced, such as はな + ち = はなぢ. When typing, use di.
