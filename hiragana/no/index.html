Hiragana の (no)

の sits in the na row, o column of the gojuon chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

の no

1 stroke

How to write の: stroke order

の has 1 stroke. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

Practise writing の by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce の

no is the n sound followed by a short, pure o. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
