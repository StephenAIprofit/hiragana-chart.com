Hiragana ね (ne)

ね sits in the na row, e column of the gojuon chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ね ne

2 strokes

How to write ね: stroke order

ね has 2 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

Practise writing ね by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce ね

ne is the n sound followed by a short, pure e. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
