Hiragana な (na)

な sits in the na row, a column of the gojuon chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

な na

4 strokes

How to write な: stroke order

な has 4 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

Practise writing な by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce な

na is the n sound followed by a short, pure a. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
