Hiragana べ (be)

べ is へ with two dots (dakuten) added. The mark changes the sound from he to be. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

べ be

3 strokes

How to write べ: stroke order

べ has 3 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next. Write へ first, then add the two dots in the top right.

Practise writing べ by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce べ

be is the b sound followed by a short, pure e. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
