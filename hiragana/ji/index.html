Hiragana じ (ji)

じ is し with two dots (dakuten) added. The mark changes the sound from shi to ji. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

じ ji

3 strokes

How to write じ: stroke order

じ has 3 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next. Write し first, then add the two dots in the top right.

Practise writing じ by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce じ

In modern Japanese じ and ぢ sound the same. じ is the normal spelling; ぢ appears mainly in compounds such as はなぢ.
