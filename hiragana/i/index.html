Hiragana い (i)

い is one of the five vowels, in the first row of the chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

い i

2 strokes

How to write い: stroke order

い has 2 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

How to pronounce い

A short ee, as in see, but clipped. Keep the lips relaxed.
