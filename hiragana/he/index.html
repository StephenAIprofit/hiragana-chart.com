Hiragana へ (he)

へ sits in the ha row, e column of the gojuon chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

へ he

1 stroke

How to write へ: stroke order

へ has 1 stroke. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

Practise writing へ by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce へ

Read he in words, but as the direction particle へ it is read e: にほんへ (nihon e).
