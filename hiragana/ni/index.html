Hiragana に (ni)

に sits in the na row, i column of the gojuon chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

に ni

3 strokes

How to write に: stroke order

に has 3 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

How to pronounce に

ni is the n sound followed by a short, pure i. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
