Hiragana きゃ (kya)

きゃ is a yoon: き (ki) plus a small ゃ. The two characters are read together as one beat, kya. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

きゃ kya

7 strokes

How to write きゃ: stroke order

き (4 strokes)

small ゃ (3 strokes)

Write き at full size, then the small ゃ in the lower right of the same square, at about half the size. Total: 7 strokes.

Practise writing きゃ by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce きゃ

Say it as one sound, not ki plus ya. Compare きゃ (kya, one beat) with きや (ki-ya, two beats).
