Hiragana ちゅ (chu)

ちゅ is a yoon: ち (chi) plus a small ゅ. The two characters are read together as one beat, chu. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ちゅ chu

4 strokes

How to write ちゅ: stroke order

ち (2 strokes)

small ゅ (2 strokes)

Write ち at full size, then the small ゅ in the lower right of the same square, at about half the size. Total: 4 strokes.

How to pronounce ちゅ

Say it as one sound, not chi plus yu. Compare ちゅ (chu, one beat) with ちゆ (chi-yu, two beats).
