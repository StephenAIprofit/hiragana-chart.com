Hiragana つ (tsu)

つ sits in the ta row, u column of the gojuon chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

つ tsu

1 stroke

How to write つ: stroke order

つ has 1 stroke. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

How to pronounce つ

Hepburn writes tsu, not tu. Start with ts, like the end of cats, then a light u.
