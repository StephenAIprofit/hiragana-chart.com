Hiragana も (mo)

も sits in the ma row, o column of the gojuon chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

も mo

3 strokes

How to write も: stroke order

も has 3 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

Practise writing も by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce も

mo is the m sound followed by a short, pure o. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
