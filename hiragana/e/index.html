Hiragana え (e)

え is one of the five vowels, in the first row of the chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

How to write え: stroke order

え has 2 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

Practise writing え by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce え

Like the e in pet. It never glides into ay.
