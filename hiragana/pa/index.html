Hiragana ぱ (pa)

ぱ is は with a small circle (handakuten) added. The mark changes the sound from ha to pa. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ぱ pa

4 strokes

How to write ぱ: stroke order

ぱ has 4 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next. Write は first, then add the circle in the top right.

Practise writing ぱ by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce ぱ

pa is the p sound followed by a short, pure a. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
