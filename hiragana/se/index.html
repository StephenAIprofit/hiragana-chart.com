Hiragana せ (se)

せ sits in the sa row, e column of the gojuon chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

せ se

3 strokes

How to write せ: stroke order

せ has 3 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

How to pronounce せ

se is the s sound followed by a short, pure e. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
