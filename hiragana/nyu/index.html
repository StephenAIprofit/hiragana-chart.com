Hiragana にゅ (nyu)

にゅ is a yoon: に (ni) plus a small ゅ. The two characters are read together as one beat, nyu. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

にゅ nyu

5 strokes

How to write にゅ: stroke order

に (3 strokes)

small ゅ (2 strokes)

Write に at full size, then the small ゅ in the lower right of the same square, at about half the size. Total: 5 strokes.

Practise writing にゅ by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce にゅ

Say it as one sound, not ni plus yu. Compare にゅ (nyu, one beat) with にゆ (ni-yu, two beats).
