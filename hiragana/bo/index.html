Hiragana ぼ (bo)

ぼ is ほ with two dots (dakuten) added. The mark changes the sound from ho to bo. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ぼ bo

6 strokes

How to write ぼ: stroke order

ぼ has 6 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next. Write ほ first, then add the two dots in the top right.

Practise writing ぼ by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce ぼ

bo is the b sound followed by a short, pure o. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
