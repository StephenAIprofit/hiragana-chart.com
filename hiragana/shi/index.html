Hiragana し (shi)

し sits in the sa row, i column of the gojuon chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

し shi

1 stroke

How to write し: stroke order

し has 1 stroke. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

Practise writing し by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce し

Hepburn writes shi, not si. The sound is close to English she, said quickly.
