Hiragana ぶ (bu)

ぶ is ふ with two dots (dakuten) added. The mark changes the sound from fu to bu. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ぶ bu

6 strokes

How to write ぶ: stroke order

ぶ has 6 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next. Write ふ first, then add the two dots in the top right.

Practise writing ぶ by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce ぶ

bu is the b sound followed by a short, pure u. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
