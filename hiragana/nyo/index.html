Hiragana · Yoon

Hiragana にょ (nyo)

にょ is a yoon: に (ni) plus a small ょ. The two characters are read together as one beat, nyo. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

にょ nyo

5 strokes

How to write にょ: stroke order

に (3 strokes)

small ょ (2 strokes)

Write に at full size, then the small ょ in the lower right of the same square, at about half the size. Total: 5 strokes.

How to pronounce にょ

Say it as one sound, not ni plus yo. Compare にょ (nyo, one beat) with によ (ni-yo, two beats).

Is にょ common?

にょ is rare in modern Hiragana. Learn to recognise it, but spend your practice time on the common sounds first.
