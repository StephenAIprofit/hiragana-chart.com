Hiragana お (o)

お is one of the five vowels, in the first row of the chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

お o

3 strokes

How to write お: stroke order

お has 3 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

Practise writing お by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce お

Like the o in more, short and without a w at the end.
