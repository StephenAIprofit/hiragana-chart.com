Hiragana ひ (hi)

ひ sits in the ha row, i column of the gojuon chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ひ hi

1 stroke

How to write ひ: stroke order

ひ has 1 stroke. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

How to pronounce ひ

A breathy h, close to the h in huge.
