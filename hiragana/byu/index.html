Hiragana びゅ (byu)

びゅ is a yoon: び (bi) plus a small ゅ. The two characters are read together as one beat, byu. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

びゅ byu

5 strokes

How to write びゅ: stroke order

び (3 strokes)

small ゅ (2 strokes)

Write び at full size, then the small ゅ in the lower right of the same square, at about half the size. Total: 5 strokes.

How to pronounce びゅ

Say it as one sound, not bi plus yu. Compare びゅ (byu, one beat) with びゆ (bi-yu, two beats).

Is びゅ common?

びゅ is rare in modern Hiragana. Learn to recognise it, but spend your practice time on the common sounds first.
