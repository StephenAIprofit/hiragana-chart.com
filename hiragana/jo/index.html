Hiragana · Yoon

Hiragana じょ (jo)

じょ is a yoon: じ (ji) plus a small ょ. The two characters are read together as one beat, jo. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

じょ jo

5 strokes

How to write じょ: stroke order

じ (3 strokes)

small ょ (2 strokes)

Write じ at full size, then the small ょ in the lower right of the same square, at about half the size. Total: 5 strokes.

How to pronounce じょ

Say it as one sound, not ji plus yo. Compare じょ (jo, one beat) with じよ (ji-yo, two beats).
