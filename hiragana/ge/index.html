Hiragana げ (ge)

げ is け with two dots (dakuten) added. The mark changes the sound from ke to ge. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

げ ge

5 strokes

How to write げ: stroke order

げ has 5 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next. Write け first, then add the two dots in the top right.

How to pronounce げ

ge is the g sound followed by a short, pure e. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
