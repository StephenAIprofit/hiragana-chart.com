Katakana quiz questions

How does the Katakana quiz work?

Each question shows a reading or a katakana and asks you to pick the match. Choose Romaji → Kana to practise recognition from sound, or Kana → Romaji to practise reading. Every answer plays the audio.

What do L1, L2, L3, and L4 mean in Katakana?

L1 is the basic 46 katakana, ア to ン. L2 adds dakuten and handakuten (ガ to ポ) for 71. L3 adds yoon such as キャ and ショ for 107. L4 is the same set as L3.

Which katakana do learners miss most?

シ and ツ, ソ and ン, and the group ク, ケ, タ. If the quiz keeps catching you on these, compare them on the Confusable kana page, then retest.

Can I practise Katakana words?

Words mode uses Hiragana spellings, so stay in Characters mode for Katakana. To see Katakana in real words, open any character on the Katakana chart: each page shows a loanword example.

Do I need an account?

No. Wrong answers return later in the same session, and nothing is saved to a server.
