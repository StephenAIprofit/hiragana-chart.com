Privacy

No account

This site has no login and no user accounts. You do not create a profile, and the site does not store your name or email unless you send a message from the About page.

Quiz data stays on your device

The Quiz keeps your score, combo, and wrong answers in memory for the current session. Closing the tab clears them. Nothing is sent to a server.

Cookies and ads

The site may show ads from Google AdSense. AdSense uses cookies to measure ad performance and to show ads that may be relevant to you. These cookies are set by Google, not by this site.

You can manage or block cookies in your browser settings. Blocking cookies does not stop the chart, quiz, or translator from working.

What the site does not do

The site does not sell personal data. It does not require an email to use the tools. The contact form on the About page is the only place that sends your email and message, and it goes only to the site owner.
