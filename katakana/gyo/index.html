Katakana · Yoon

Katakana ギョ (gyo)

ギョ is a yoon: ギ (gi) plus a small ョ. The two characters are read together as one beat, gyo. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ギョ gyo

8 strokes

How to write ギョ: stroke order

ギ (5 strokes)

small ョ (3 strokes)

Write ギ at full size, then the small ョ in the lower right of the same square, at about half the size. Total: 8 strokes.

How to pronounce ギョ

Say it as one sound, not gi plus yo. Compare ギョ (gyo, one beat) with ギヨ (gi-yo, two beats).

Is ギョ common?

ギョ is rare in modern Katakana. Learn to recognise it, but spend your practice time on the common sounds first.
