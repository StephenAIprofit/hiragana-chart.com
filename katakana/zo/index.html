Katakana ゾ (zo)

ゾ is ソ with two dots (dakuten) added. The mark changes the sound from so to zo. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ゾ zo

4 strokes

How to write ゾ: stroke order

ゾ has 4 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next. Write ソ first, then add the two dots in the top right.

How to pronounce ゾ

zo is the z sound followed by a short, pure o. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
