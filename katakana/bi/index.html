Katakana ビ (bi)

ビ is ヒ with two dots (dakuten) added. The mark changes the sound from hi to bi. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ビ bi

4 strokes

How to write ビ: stroke order

ビ has 4 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next. Write ヒ first, then add the two dots in the top right.

How to pronounce ビ

bi is the b sound followed by a short, pure i. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
