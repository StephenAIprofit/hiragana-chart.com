Katakana ギ (gi)

ギ is キ with two dots (dakuten) added. The mark changes the sound from ki to gi. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ギ gi

5 strokes

How to write ギ: stroke order

ギ has 5 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next. Write キ first, then add the two dots in the top right.

Practise writing ギ by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce ギ

gi is the g sound followed by a short, pure i. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
