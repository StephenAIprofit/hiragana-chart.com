Katakana ピョ (pyo)

ピョ is a yoon: ピ (pi) plus a small ョ. The two characters are read together as one beat, pyo. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ピョ pyo

6 strokes

How to write ピョ: stroke order

ピ (3 strokes)

small ョ (3 strokes)

Write ピ at full size, then the small ョ in the lower right of the same square, at about half the size. Total: 6 strokes.

Practise writing ピョ by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce ピョ

Say it as one sound, not pi plus yo. Compare ピョ (pyo, one beat) with ピヨ (pi-yo, two beats).

Is ピョ common?

ピョ is rare in modern Katakana. Learn to recognise it, but spend your practice time on the common sounds first.
