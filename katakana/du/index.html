Katakana ヅ (zu)

ヅ is ツ with two dots (dakuten) added. The mark changes the sound from tsu to zu. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ヅ zu

5 strokes

How to write ヅ: stroke order

ヅ has 5 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next. Write ツ first, then add the two dots in the top right.

How to pronounce ヅ

Read zu, the same sound as ズ. It is very rare in katakana; loanwords use ズ. When typing, use du.

Is ヅ common?

ヅ is rare in modern Katakana. Learn to recognise it, but spend your practice time on the common sounds first.
