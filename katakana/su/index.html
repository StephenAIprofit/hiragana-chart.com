Katakana ス (su)

ス sits in the sa row, u column of the gojuon chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ス su

2 strokes

How to write ス: stroke order

ス has 2 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

Practise writing ス by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce ス

su is the s sound followed by a short, pure u. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
