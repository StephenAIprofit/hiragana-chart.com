Katakana · Yoon

Katakana ニュ (nyu)

ニュ is a yoon: ニ (ni) plus a small ュ. The two characters are read together as one beat, nyu. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ニュ nyu

4 strokes

How to write ニュ: stroke order

ニ (2 strokes)

small ュ (2 strokes)

Write ニ at full size, then the small ュ in the lower right of the same square, at about half the size. Total: 4 strokes.

How to pronounce ニュ

Say it as one sound, not ni plus yu. Compare ニュ (nyu, one beat) with ニユ (ni-yu, two beats).
