Katakana ラ (ra)

ラ sits in the ra row, a column of the gojuon chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ラ ra

How to write ラ: stroke order

ラ has 2 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

Practise writing ラ by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce ラ

Japanese r is a quick tap of the tongue tip behind the teeth, between English r, l, and d.
