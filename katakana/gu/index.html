Katakana グ (gu)

グ is ク with two dots (dakuten) added. The mark changes the sound from ku to gu. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

グ gu

4 strokes

How to write グ: stroke order

グ has 4 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next. Write ク first, then add the two dots in the top right.

Practise writing グ by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce グ

gu is the g sound followed by a short, pure u. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
