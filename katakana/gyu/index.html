Katakana · Yoon

Katakana ギュ (gyu)

ギュ is a yoon: ギ (gi) plus a small ュ. The two characters are read together as one beat, gyu. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ギュ gyu

7 strokes

How to write ギュ: stroke order

ギ (5 strokes)

small ュ (2 strokes)

Write ギ at full size, then the small ュ in the lower right of the same square, at about half the size. Total: 7 strokes.

How to pronounce ギュ

Say it as one sound, not gi plus yu. Compare ギュ (gyu, one beat) with ギユ (gi-yu, two beats).

Is ギュ common?

ギュ is rare in modern Katakana. Learn to recognise it, but spend your practice time on the common sounds first.
