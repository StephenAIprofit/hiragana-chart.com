Katakana ア (a)

ア is one of the five vowels, in the first row of the chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ア a

2 strokes

How to write ア: stroke order

ア has 2 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

How to pronounce ア

A short, open a, like the a in father but clipped. Japanese vowels are pure: no glide at the end.
