Katakana ン (n)

ン is the last character of the chart and stands on its own row. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ン n

2 strokes

How to write ン: stroke order

ン has 2 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

Practise writing ン by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce ン

The only kana that is just a consonant. It takes one full beat: パン is two beats, pa-n. Before p, b, and m it sounds like m.
