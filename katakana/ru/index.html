Katakana ル (ru)

ル sits in the ra row, u column of the gojuon chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ル ru

2 strokes

How to write ル: stroke order

ル has 2 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

Practise writing ル by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce ル

Japanese r is a quick tap of the tongue tip behind the teeth, between English r, l, and d.
