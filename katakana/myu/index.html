Katakana · Yoon

Katakana ミュ (myu)

ミュ is a yoon: ミ (mi) plus a small ュ. The two characters are read together as one beat, myu. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ミュ myu

5 strokes

How to write ミュ: stroke order

ミ (3 strokes)

small ュ (2 strokes)

Write ミ at full size, then the small ュ in the lower right of the same square, at about half the size. Total: 5 strokes.

How to pronounce ミュ

Say it as one sound, not mi plus yu. Compare ミュ (myu, one beat) with ミユ (mi-yu, two beats).
