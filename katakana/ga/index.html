Katakana ガ (ga)

ガ is カ with two dots (dakuten) added. The mark changes the sound from ka to ga. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ガ ga

4 strokes

How to write ガ: stroke order

ガ has 4 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next. Write カ first, then add the two dots in the top right.

How to pronounce ガ

ga is the g sound followed by a short, pure a. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
