Katakana ミ (mi)

ミ sits in the ma row, i column of the gojuon chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ミ mi

3 strokes

How to write ミ: stroke order

ミ has 3 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

How to pronounce ミ

mi is the m sound followed by a short, pure i. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
