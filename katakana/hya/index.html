Katakana · Yoon

Katakana ヒャ (hya)

ヒャ is a yoon: ヒ (hi) plus a small ャ. The two characters are read together as one beat, hya. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ヒャ hya

4 strokes

How to write ヒャ: stroke order

ヒ (2 strokes)

small ャ (2 strokes)

Write ヒ at full size, then the small ャ in the lower right of the same square, at about half the size. Total: 4 strokes.

How to pronounce ヒャ

Say it as one sound, not hi plus ya. Compare ヒャ (hya, one beat) with ヒヤ (hi-ya, two beats).

Is ヒャ common?

ヒャ is rare in modern Katakana. Learn to recognise it, but spend your practice time on the common sounds first.
