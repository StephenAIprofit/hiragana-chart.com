Katakana フ (fu)

フ sits in the ha row, u column of the gojuon chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

フ fu

1 stroke

How to write フ: stroke order

フ has 1 stroke. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

Practise writing フ by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce フ

Not an English f. Blow air gently between both lips, as if cooling soup. It sits between h and f.
