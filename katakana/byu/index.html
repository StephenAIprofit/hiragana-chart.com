Katakana · Yoon

Katakana ビュ (byu)

ビュ is a yoon: ビ (bi) plus a small ュ. The two characters are read together as one beat, byu. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ビュ byu

6 strokes

How to write ビュ: stroke order

ビ (4 strokes)

small ュ (2 strokes)

Write ビ at full size, then the small ュ in the lower right of the same square, at about half the size. Total: 6 strokes.

How to pronounce ビュ

Say it as one sound, not bi plus yu. Compare ビュ (byu, one beat) with ビユ (bi-yu, two beats).
