Katakana シュ (shu)

シュ is a yoon: シ (shi) plus a small ュ. The two characters are read together as one beat, shu. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

シュ shu

5 strokes

How to write シュ: stroke order

シ (3 strokes)

small ュ (2 strokes)

Write シ at full size, then the small ュ in the lower right of the same square, at about half the size. Total: 5 strokes.

Practise writing シュ by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce シュ

Say it as one sound, not shi plus yu. Compare シュ (shu, one beat) with シユ (shi-yu, two beats).
