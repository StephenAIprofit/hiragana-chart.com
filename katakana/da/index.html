Katakana ダ (da)

ダ is タ with two dots (dakuten) added. The mark changes the sound from ta to da. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ダ da

5 strokes

How to write ダ: stroke order

ダ has 5 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next. Write タ first, then add the two dots in the top right.

Practise writing ダ by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce ダ

da is the d sound followed by a short, pure a. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
