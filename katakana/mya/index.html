Katakana ミャ (mya)

ミャ is a yoon: ミ (mi) plus a small ャ. The two characters are read together as one beat, mya. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ミャ mya

5 strokes

How to write ミャ: stroke order

ミ (3 strokes)

small ャ (2 strokes)

Write ミ at full size, then the small ャ in the lower right of the same square, at about half the size. Total: 5 strokes.

Practise writing ミャ by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce ミャ

Say it as one sound, not mi plus ya. Compare ミャ (mya, one beat) with ミヤ (mi-ya, two beats).
