Katakana サ (sa)

サ sits in the sa row, a column of the gojuon chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

サ sa

3 strokes

How to write サ: stroke order

サ has 3 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

How to pronounce サ

sa is the s sound followed by a short, pure a. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
