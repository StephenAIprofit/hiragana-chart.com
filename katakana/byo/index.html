Katakana ビョ (byo)

ビョ is a yoon: ビ (bi) plus a small ョ. The two characters are read together as one beat, byo. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ビョ byo

7 strokes

How to write ビョ: stroke order

ビ (4 strokes)

small ョ (3 strokes)

Write ビ at full size, then the small ョ in the lower right of the same square, at about half the size. Total: 7 strokes.

Practise writing ビョ by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce ビョ

Say it as one sound, not bi plus yo. Compare ビョ (byo, one beat) with ビヨ (bi-yo, two beats).

Is ビョ common?

ビョ is rare in modern Katakana. Learn to recognise it, but spend your practice time on the common sounds first.
