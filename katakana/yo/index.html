Katakana ヨ (yo)

ヨ sits in the ya row, o column of the gojuon chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ヨ yo

3 strokes

How to write ヨ: stroke order

ヨ has 3 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

How to pronounce ヨ

yo is the y sound followed by a short, pure o. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
