Katakana プ (pu)

プ is フ with a small circle (handakuten) added. The mark changes the sound from fu to pu. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

プ pu

2 strokes

How to write プ: stroke order

プ has 2 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next. Write フ first, then add the circle in the top right.

How to pronounce プ

pu is the p sound followed by a short, pure u. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
