Katakana ニャ (nya)

ニャ is a yoon: ニ (ni) plus a small ャ. The two characters are read together as one beat, nya. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ニャ nya

4 strokes

How to write ニャ: stroke order

ニ (2 strokes)

small ャ (2 strokes)

Write ニ at full size, then the small ャ in the lower right of the same square, at about half the size. Total: 4 strokes.

Practise writing ニャ by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce ニャ

Say it as one sound, not ni plus ya. Compare ニャ (nya, one beat) with ニヤ (ni-ya, two beats).

Is ニャ common?

ニャ is rare in modern Katakana. Learn to recognise it, but spend your practice time on the common sounds first.
