Katakana ズ (zu)

ズ is ス with two dots (dakuten) added. The mark changes the sound from su to zu. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ズ zu

4 strokes

How to write ズ: stroke order

ズ has 4 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next. Write ス first, then add the two dots in the top right.

Practise writing ズ by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce ズ

ズ is the normal katakana spelling of zu. ヅ sounds the same but is very rare.
