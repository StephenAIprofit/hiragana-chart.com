Katakana コ (ko)

コ sits in the ka row, o column of the gojuon chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

コ ko

2 strokes

How to write コ: stroke order

コ has 2 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

Practise writing コ by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce コ

ko is the k sound followed by a short, pure o. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
