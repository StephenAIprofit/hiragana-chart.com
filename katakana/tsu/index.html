Katakana ツ (tsu)

ツ sits in the ta row, u column of the gojuon chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ツ tsu

3 strokes

How to write ツ: stroke order

ツ has 3 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

Practise writing ツ by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

Get the Workbook · $9.90

How to pronounce ツ

Hepburn writes tsu, not tu. Start with ts, like the end of cats, then a light u.
