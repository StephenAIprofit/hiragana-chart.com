Katakana · Yoon

Katakana キャ (kya)

キャ is a yoon: キ (ki) plus a small ャ. The two characters are read together as one beat, kya. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

キャ kya

5 strokes

How to write キャ: stroke order

キ (3 strokes)

small ャ (2 strokes)

Write キ at full size, then the small ャ in the lower right of the same square, at about half the size. Total: 5 strokes.

How to pronounce キャ

Say it as one sound, not ki plus ya. Compare キャ (kya, one beat) with キヤ (ki-ya, two beats).
