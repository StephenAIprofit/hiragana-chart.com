Katakana ヂョ (jo)

ヂョ is a yoon: ヂ (ji) plus a small ョ. The two characters are read together as one beat, jo. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ヂョ jo

8 strokes

How to write ヂョ: stroke order

ヂ (5 strokes)

small ョ (3 strokes)

Write ヂ at full size, then the small ョ in the lower right of the same square, at about half the size. Total: 8 strokes.

Practise writing ヂョ by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce ヂョ

Read jo, the same as ジョ. This spelling is almost never used.

Is ヂョ common?

ヂョ is rare in modern Katakana. Learn to recognise it, but spend your practice time on the common sounds first.
