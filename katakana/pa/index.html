Katakana パ (pa)

パ is ハ with a small circle (handakuten) added. The mark changes the sound from ha to pa. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

パ pa

3 strokes

How to write パ: stroke order

パ has 3 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next. Write ハ first, then add the circle in the top right.

How to pronounce パ

pa is the p sound followed by a short, pure a. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
