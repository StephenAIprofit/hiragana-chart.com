Katakana シ (shi)

シ sits in the sa row, i column of the gojuon chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

シ shi

3 strokes

How to write シ: stroke order

シ has 3 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

Practise writing シ by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce シ

Hepburn writes shi, not si. The sound is close to English she, said quickly.
