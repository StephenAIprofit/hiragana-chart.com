Katakana ヌ (nu)

ヌ sits in the na row, u column of the gojuon chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ヌ nu

2 strokes

How to write ヌ: stroke order

ヌ has 2 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

How to pronounce ヌ

nu is the n sound followed by a short, pure u. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
