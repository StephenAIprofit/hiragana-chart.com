Katakana ウ (u)

ウ is one of the five vowels, in the first row of the chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ウ u

3 strokes

How to write ウ: stroke order

ウ has 3 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

How to pronounce ウ

An unrounded u: say oo with relaxed, not pushed-out, lips. It is often quiet between voiceless consonants, as in スキー (sukii, sounds like skii).
