Katakana · Yoon

Katakana ヂャ (ja)

ヂャ is a yoon: ヂ (ji) plus a small ャ. The two characters are read together as one beat, ja. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ヂャ ja

7 strokes

How to write ヂャ: stroke order

ヂ (5 strokes)

small ャ (2 strokes)

Write ヂ at full size, then the small ャ in the lower right of the same square, at about half the size. Total: 7 strokes.

How to pronounce ヂャ

Read ja, the same as ジャ. This spelling is almost never used.

Is ヂャ common?

ヂャ is rare in modern Katakana. Learn to recognise it, but spend your practice time on the common sounds first.
