Katakana ヒュ (hyu)

ヒュ is a yoon: ヒ (hi) plus a small ュ. The two characters are read together as one beat, hyu. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ヒュ hyu

4 strokes

How to write ヒュ: stroke order

ヒ (2 strokes)

small ュ (2 strokes)

Write ヒ at full size, then the small ュ in the lower right of the same square, at about half the size. Total: 4 strokes.

Practise writing ヒュ by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce ヒュ

Say it as one sound, not hi plus yu. Compare ヒュ (hyu, one beat) with ヒユ (hi-yu, two beats).
