Katakana セ (se)

セ sits in the sa row, e column of the gojuon chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

セ se

2 strokes

How to write セ: stroke order

セ has 2 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

How to pronounce セ

se is the s sound followed by a short, pure e. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
