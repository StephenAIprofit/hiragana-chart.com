Katakana ド (do)

ド is ト with two dots (dakuten) added. The mark changes the sound from to to do. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ド do

4 strokes

How to write ド: stroke order

ド has 4 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next. Write ト first, then add the two dots in the top right.

How to pronounce ド

do is the d sound followed by a short, pure o. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
