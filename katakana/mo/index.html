Katakana モ (mo)

モ sits in the ma row, o column of the gojuon chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

モ mo

3 strokes

How to write モ: stroke order

モ has 3 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

How to pronounce モ

mo is the m sound followed by a short, pure o. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
