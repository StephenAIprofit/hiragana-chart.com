Katakana ゴ (go)

ゴ is コ with two dots (dakuten) added. The mark changes the sound from ko to go. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ゴ go

4 strokes

How to write ゴ: stroke order

ゴ has 4 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next. Write コ first, then add the two dots in the top right.

Practise writing ゴ by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce ゴ

go is the g sound followed by a short, pure o. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
