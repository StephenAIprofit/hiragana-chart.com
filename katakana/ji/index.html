Katakana ジ (ji)

ジ is シ with two dots (dakuten) added. The mark changes the sound from shi to ji. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ジ ji

5 strokes

How to write ジ: stroke order

ジ has 5 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next. Write シ first, then add the two dots in the top right.

How to pronounce ジ

ジ is the normal katakana spelling of ji. ヂ sounds the same but is very rare.
