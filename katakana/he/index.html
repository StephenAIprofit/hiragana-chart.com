Katakana ヘ (he)

ヘ sits in the ha row, e column of the gojuon chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ヘ he

1 stroke

How to write ヘ: stroke order

ヘ has 1 stroke. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

How to pronounce ヘ

Always read he. The particle reading e only applies to the hiragana へ.
