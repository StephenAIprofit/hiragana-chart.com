Katakana ナ (na)

ナ sits in the na row, a column of the gojuon chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ナ na

2 strokes

How to write ナ: stroke order

ナ has 2 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

How to pronounce ナ

na is the n sound followed by a short, pure a. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
