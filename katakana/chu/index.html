Katakana · Yoon

Katakana チュ (chu)

チュ is a yoon: チ (chi) plus a small ュ. The two characters are read together as one beat, chu. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

チュ chu

5 strokes

How to write チュ: stroke order

チ (3 strokes)

small ュ (2 strokes)

Write チ at full size, then the small ュ in the lower right of the same square, at about half the size. Total: 5 strokes.

How to pronounce チュ

Say it as one sound, not chi plus yu. Compare チュ (chu, one beat) with チユ (chi-yu, two beats).
