Katakana リャ (rya)

リャ is a yoon: リ (ri) plus a small ャ. The two characters are read together as one beat, rya. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

リャ rya

4 strokes

How to write リャ: stroke order

リ (2 strokes)

small ャ (2 strokes)

Write リ at full size, then the small ャ in the lower right of the same square, at about half the size. Total: 4 strokes.

Practise writing リャ by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce リャ

Say it as one sound, not ri plus ya. Compare リャ (rya, one beat) with リヤ (ri-ya, two beats).

Is リャ common?

リャ is rare in modern Katakana. Learn to recognise it, but spend your practice time on the common sounds first.
