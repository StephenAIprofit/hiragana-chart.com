Katakana メ (me)

メ sits in the ma row, e column of the gojuon chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

メ me

2 strokes

How to write メ: stroke order

メ has 2 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

Practise writing メ by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce メ

me is the m sound followed by a short, pure e. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
