Katakana テ (te)

テ sits in the ta row, e column of the gojuon chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

テ te

3 strokes

How to write テ: stroke order

テ has 3 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

Practise writing テ by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce テ

te is the t sound followed by a short, pure e. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
