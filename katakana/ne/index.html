Katakana ネ (ne)

ネ sits in the na row, e column of the gojuon chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ネ ne

4 strokes

How to write ネ: stroke order

ネ has 4 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

Practise writing ネ by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce ネ

ne is the n sound followed by a short, pure e. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
