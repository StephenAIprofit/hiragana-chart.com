Katakana ロ (ro)

ロ sits in the ra row, o column of the gojuon chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ロ ro

3 strokes

How to write ロ: stroke order

ロ has 3 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

How to pronounce ロ

Japanese r is a quick tap of the tongue tip behind the teeth, between English r, l, and d.
