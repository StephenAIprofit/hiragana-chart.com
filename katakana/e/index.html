Katakana エ (e)

エ is one of the five vowels, in the first row of the chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

エ e

How to write エ: stroke order

エ has 3 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

Practise writing エ by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.
