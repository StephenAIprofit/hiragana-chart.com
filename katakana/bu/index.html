Katakana ブ (bu)

ブ is フ with two dots (dakuten) added. The mark changes the sound from fu to bu. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ブ bu

3 strokes

How to write ブ: stroke order

ブ has 3 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next. Write フ first, then add the two dots in the top right.

Practise writing ブ by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce ブ

bu is the b sound followed by a short, pure u. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
