Katakana バ (ba)

バ is ハ with two dots (dakuten) added. The mark changes the sound from ha to ba. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

バ ba

4 strokes

How to write バ: stroke order

バ has 4 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next. Write ハ first, then add the two dots in the top right.

How to pronounce バ

ba is the b sound followed by a short, pure a. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
