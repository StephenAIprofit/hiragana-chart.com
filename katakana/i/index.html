Katakana イ (i)

イ is one of the five vowels, in the first row of the chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

イ i

2 strokes

How to write イ: stroke order

イ has 2 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

Practise writing イ by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce イ

A short ee, as in see, but clipped. Keep the lips relaxed.
