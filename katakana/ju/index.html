Katakana ジュ (ju)

ジュ is a yoon: ジ (ji) plus a small ュ. The two characters are read together as one beat, ju. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ジュ ju

7 strokes

How to write ジュ: stroke order

ジ (5 strokes)

small ュ (2 strokes)

Write ジ at full size, then the small ュ in the lower right of the same square, at about half the size. Total: 7 strokes.

Practise writing ジュ by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce ジュ

Say it as one sound, not ji plus yu. Compare ジュ (ju, one beat) with ジユ (ji-yu, two beats).
