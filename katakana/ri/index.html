Katakana リ (ri)

リ sits in the ra row, i column of the gojuon chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

リ ri

2 strokes

How to write リ: stroke order

リ has 2 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

How to pronounce リ

Japanese r is a quick tap of the tongue tip behind the teeth, between English r, l, and d.
