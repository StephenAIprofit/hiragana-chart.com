Katakana ザ (za)

ザ is サ with two dots (dakuten) added. The mark changes the sound from sa to za. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ザ za

5 strokes

How to write ザ: stroke order

ザ has 5 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next. Write サ first, then add the two dots in the top right.

How to pronounce ザ

za is the z sound followed by a short, pure a. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
