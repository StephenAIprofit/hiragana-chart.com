Katakana · Dakuten

Katakana ボ (bo)

ボ is ホ with two dots (dakuten) added. The mark changes the sound from ho to bo. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ボ bo

6 strokes

How to write ボ: stroke order

ボ has 6 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next. Write ホ first, then add the two dots in the top right.

How to pronounce ボ

bo is the b sound followed by a short, pure o. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
