Katakana ヲ (wo)

ヲ sits in the wa row, o column of the gojuon chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

How to write ヲ: stroke order

ヲ has 3 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

Practise writing ヲ by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce ヲ

Read o, and very rare. ヲ appears mostly in old texts and in text written entirely in katakana; normal writing uses the hiragana を.

Is ヲ common?

ヲ is rare in modern Katakana. Learn to recognise it, but spend your practice time on the common sounds first.
