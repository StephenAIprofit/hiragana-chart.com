Katakana · Handakuten

Katakana ポ (po)

ポ is ホ with a small circle (handakuten) added. The mark changes the sound from ho to po. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ポ po

5 strokes

How to write ポ: stroke order

ポ has 5 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next. Write ホ first, then add the circle in the top right.

How to pronounce ポ

po is the p sound followed by a short, pure o. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
