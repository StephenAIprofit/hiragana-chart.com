Katakana ク (ku)

ク sits in the ka row, u column of the gojuon chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ク ku

2 strokes

How to write ク: stroke order

ク has 2 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

Practise writing ク by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce ク

ku is the k sound followed by a short, pure u. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
