Katakana マ (ma)

マ sits in the ma row, a column of the gojuon chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

マ ma

How to write マ: stroke order

マ has 2 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

Practise writing マ by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce マ

ma is the m sound followed by a short, pure a. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
