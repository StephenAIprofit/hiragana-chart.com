Katakana ム (mu)

ム sits in the ma row, u column of the gojuon chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ム mu

2 strokes

How to write ム: stroke order

ム has 2 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

How to pronounce ム

mu is the m sound followed by a short, pure u. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
