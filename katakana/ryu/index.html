Katakana · Yoon

Katakana リュ (ryu)

リュ is a yoon: リ (ri) plus a small ュ. The two characters are read together as one beat, ryu. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

リュ ryu

4 strokes

How to write リュ: stroke order

リ (2 strokes)

small ュ (2 strokes)

Write リ at full size, then the small ュ in the lower right of the same square, at about half the size. Total: 4 strokes.

How to pronounce リュ

Say it as one sound, not ri plus yu. Compare リュ (ryu, one beat) with リユ (ri-yu, two beats).
