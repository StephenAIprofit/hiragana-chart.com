Katakana ホ (ho)

ホ sits in the ha row, o column of the gojuon chart. Click play to hear it, and watch the numbered strokes below.

ホ ho

4 strokes

How to write ホ: stroke order

ホ has 4 strokes. Write them in the numbered order: generally top to bottom and left to right, and finish each stroke before starting the next.

Practise writing ホ by hand, then keep going with the rest of the kana in the workbook.

How to pronounce ホ

ho is the h sound followed by a short, pure o. Keep the vowel short and do not let it glide.
