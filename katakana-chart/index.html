Katakana dakuten and handakuten

Dakuten add two dots (ガ ザ ダ バ). Handakuten add a circle (パ ピ プ ペ ポ). In modern Japanese, ヂ is read ji and ヅ is read zu.

Katakana yoon (contracted sounds)

Yoon is a large kana plus a small ャ, ュ, or ョ. The small character is not an independent sound. ヂャ, ヂュ, and ヂョ are rare; they use the same sound as ジャ, ジュ, and ジョ.

Common questions

How do I read a Katakana chart?

The same way as the Hiragana chart: left to right, top to bottom, one consonant per row. ア イ ウ エ オ is the vowel row, カ キ ク ケ コ is the ka row. Every Katakana has a Hiragana partner with the same sound, so if you know あ you already know how ア sounds.

When is Katakana used instead of Hiragana?

Katakana writes loanwords (コーヒー, coffee; テレビ, television), foreign names (ロンドン, London), animal and plant names in science, sound effects, and words written for emphasis, much like italics. Native words and grammar endings are written in Hiragana.

How many Katakana are there?

46 basic characters, from ア to ン. Dakuten and Handakuten add 25 (ガ to ポ), for 71. Yoon such as キャ, シュ, and チョ add 36, for 107. Loanwords also use extra combinations such as ファ, ティ, and ウィ to write sounds that Japanese does not have.

How are long vowels written in Katakana?

With the long-vowel mark ー, called chouon. It doubles the length of the vowel before it: コーヒー is ko-o-hi-i, four beats. Hiragana does not use ー in normal writing; it adds a vowel instead, as in おかあさん.

Which Katakana are easiest to confuse?

シ (shi) and ツ (tsu), and ソ (so) and ン (n). For シ and ン the last stroke rises from the bottom left; for ツ and ソ it falls from the top. ク, ケ, and タ, and コ and ユ, are also easy to mix up. See Confusable kana for side-by-side drills.

Should I learn Hiragana or Katakana first?

Learn Hiragana first: it is used in every sentence. Start Katakana once the basic 46 Hiragana are comfortable. Because the sounds are the same, Katakana usually takes about half as long. Then test yourself in the Katakana quiz.
