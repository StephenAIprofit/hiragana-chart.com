How this Kanji translator works

Draw a Kanji to look it up by handwriting. Or type English, Romaji, or kana. English matches the meaning. Romaji is converted to Hiragana. Kana matches the reading. Results show the Kanji, reading, and English.

The translator covers English to Kanji, Romaji to Kanji, and Kanji to Hiragana or Romaji. Use the Hiragana chart for the 46 basic characters, and the Hiragana quiz to practice them.
